## What surprised

- The system drive was full during the session, which stopped every Go link step. The Go build cache was cleared
  and caches and temporary files were redirected to another drive for the rest of the session.
- `lefthook` from the reference `tools/go.mod` did not build with Go 1.27 until `go-json-experiment/json` was bumped.
- The first `dev/toolfetch` truncated the extracted OpenTofu binary silently at its download cap; the unit test of a
  fake archive could not have shown it, running the tool did. It now fails on overflow, with a test.
- The catch-all route of the router hid the automatic `405`; explicit method-less patterns restore it.
- `tofu validate` and `tofu test` passed on the first run; the checks that mattered were the `expect_failures` cases.
